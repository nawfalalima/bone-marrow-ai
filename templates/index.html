About

About BoneMarrow AI

BoneMarrow AI is an educational and research application demonstrating deep learning-based image classification using a trained ResNet18 model. The system is designed to process bone marrow microscopic images and classify them into five categories using PyTorch and computer vision techniques.

This project highlights ResNet18, image classification, PyTorch, five-class classification, and the analysis of bone marrow microscopic images for research and learning purposes.
